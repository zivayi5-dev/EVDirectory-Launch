What is an OZEV-Approved EV Charger Installation Company?

OZEV-approved engineer carrying out commercial EV charger in

An OZEV-approved EV charger installation company is certified by the Office for Zero Emission Vehicles to carry out compliant EV charger installations and manage government grant applications. Only OZEV-approved installers can claim the Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS) grant on your behalf, deducting it directly from your invoice. All companies in this directory hold current OZEV approval.

How the Workplace Charging Scheme Grant Works

Workplace Charging Scheme WCS grant for commercial EV charge

The Workplace Charging Scheme provides UK businesses, charities and public sector organisations with up to 75% off the cost of commercial EV charger installation — capped at £500 per socket and up to 40 sockets per applicant. Your OZEV-approved installer manages the entire application, meaning you simply pay the reduced amount on the day.
