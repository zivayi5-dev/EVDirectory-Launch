Why OZEV Approval Matters

OZEV-approved status is the UK government's certification for EV charger installation companies. Only OZEV-approved installers can manage Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS) grant applications on your behalf — deducting up to £500 per socket from your installation invoice. Using a non-approved installer means losing access to this significant grant funding. Our directory guarantees every company listed is OZEV-approved.
