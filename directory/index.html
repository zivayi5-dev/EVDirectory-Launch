What Makes an EV Charger Installation Company OZEV-Approved?

OZEV-approved commercial EV charger installation companies U

OZEV approval means the installation company is certified by the UK government's Office for Zero Emission Vehicles to carry out compliant EV charge point installations. Approved companies must hold relevant electrical qualifications (typically NICEIC or NAPIT), carry public liability insurance, and complete OZEV's registration process. Only OZEV-approved companies can apply for the Workplace Charging Scheme grant and the LEVI fund on behalf of their clients.

How to Choose the Right Commercial EV Charger Installation Company

When selecting a commercial EV charger installation company, look for: current OZEV approval, experience with your property type (workplace, fleet depot, car park, or public sector), ability to handle DNO notifications, and transparent pricing. All companies in this directory meet the OZEV requirement. Request at least three free quotes and ask each installer about WCS grant eligibility for your site.

Workplace Charging Scheme: How Your Installer Claims the Grant

certified OZEV-approved ev charger installation company cred

Under the Workplace Charging Scheme, your OZEV-approved EV charger installation company applies for the grant on your behalf. The grant value (up to £500/socket, max 40 sockets) is deducted directly from your invoice — you never need to claim it yourself. Your installer submits the required OZEV documentation and you receive the discounted installation price on the day.
