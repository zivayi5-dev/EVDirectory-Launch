Workplace Charging Scheme 2026: The Complete Business Guide

Complete guide to the Workplace Charging Scheme 2026. Claim up to £500 per socket, OZEV requirements and step-by-step application.

The Workplace Charging Scheme 2026 is the UK government's primary grant for commercial EV charger installation — providing businesses with up to £500 per socket. This complete guide covers eligibility, application steps, and how to find an OZEV-approved installer who can manage the entire process on your behalf.

The Workplace Charging Scheme provides UK businesses with a grant of up to 75% off EV charge point installation — up to £500 per socket, maximum 40 sockets per applicant from April 2026.

Who is eligible?

Any UK business, registered charity or public sector organisation. Your installer manages the application and deducts the grant from your invoice on the day of installation.

Grant amounts from April 2026

Up to £500 per socket covering up to 40 sockets per applicant. Multiple sites can each claim independently.

What is the Workplace Charging Scheme?

The Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS) is a UK government grant programme that reduces the upfront cost of commercial EV charger installation for eligible organisations. Administered by OZEV, the scheme provides businesses, charities and public sector bodies with up to 75% off the cost of purchasing and installing EV charge points. From April 2026, the maximum grant value increased to £500 per socket. The grant applies to both tethered and untethered charge points and covers workplace, fleet depot and staff car park installations.

Who is Eligible for the WCS Grant in 2026?

wcs grant ev charger installation uk application process for businesses

Any UK-registered business, registered charity or public sector organisation is eligible for the WCS grant, provided they: have dedicated off-street parking at the installation site, use an OZEV-approved installer to carry out the installation, are not a sole trader using the charger primarily for private use, and apply before the scheme's current funding window closes. The scheme covers multiple sites — each site can apply independently for up to 40 sockets.

How Much Can You Claim? WCS Grant Amounts from April 2026

From April 2026, the WCS grant covers up to 75% of the combined cost of purchasing and installing EV charge points, up to a maximum of £500 per socket. There is no minimum grant amount. For a workplace installing 10 x 7kW smart chargers, the maximum WCS saving would be £5,000 deducted directly from your installation invoice.
