Home EV Charger Installation Guide UK 2026: Costs, Grants & What to Expect

Everything UK homeowners need to know about getting a home EV charger installed — from costs and OZEV grants to choosing the right 7kW smart charger.

📅 Published April 2026 ⏱ 8 min read 🔑 home ev charger installation

If you've recently bought an electric vehicle — or you're about to — home EV charger installation is almost certainly your next step. Charging your EV from a standard 3-pin socket is slow and inefficient. A dedicated 7kW home wall charger can fully charge most EVs overnight and costs significantly less to run than public charging. This guide covers everything UK homeowners need to know about getting a home EV charger installed in 2026.

How Much Does Home EV Charger Installation Cost in the UK?

The cost of home EV charger installation in the UK typically ranges from £800 to £1,400 for a standard 7kW smart charger, including hardware and labour. The exact price depends on the charger brand, cable run length, and whether any consumer unit upgrades are required. Premium brands such as Ohme, myenergi Zappi, and Wallbox tend to sit at the higher end of that range but offer superior smart features.

With the government's OZEV Home Charging Grant (for renters and flat owners), eligible applicants can claim up to £350 off the cost of installation. This grant does not currently apply to homeowners with driveways, but the Workplace Charging Scheme (WCS) — worth up to £500 per socket — is available to businesses and organisations installing chargers at workplace premises.

Do I Need Planning Permission for a Home EV Charger?

In most cases, no planning permission is required for domestic home EV charger installation in England, Scotland and Wales. Wall-mounted chargers fall under Permitted Development rights, meaning you can install them without applying to your local council — provided the charger does not protrude more than 0.2 metres from the wall surface. Listed buildings and properties in designated areas may have additional restrictions; your installer will advise.

7kW vs 22kW: Which Home EV Charger Is Right for You?

For the vast majority of UK homes, a 7kW single-phase charger is the correct choice. A 7kW charger can add approximately 25–30 miles of range per hour, meaning a typical 60kWh EV battery charges fully overnight in around 8–9 hours. This covers almost all daily driving patterns without any compromise.

A 22kW three-phase charger is only possible if your property has three-phase electricity supply — rare in UK residential properties. If you have three-phase supply and a high-mileage driving pattern, a 22kW charger can charge a compatible EV in under 3 hours. Your OZEV-approved installer will assess your supply before recommending.

What Happens During Home EV Charger Installation?

A typical home EV charger installation takes between 2–4 hours and follows this process:

  1. Pre-installation survey (in-person or video) — your installer assesses your consumer unit, cable route and parking position
  2. DNO notification — for chargers above 3.68kW, your installer notifies your Distribution Network Operator (this is handled automatically)
  3. Installation — the charger is mounted, cabled back to the consumer unit, and a new dedicated circuit is installed
  4. Commissioning — the charger is tested, app connected, and smart tariff set up if applicable
  5. Handover — you receive an installation certificate and warranty documentation
