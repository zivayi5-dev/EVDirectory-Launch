How to Choose Commercial EV Charger — 7kW vs 22kW vs Rapid DC: Which Commercial EV Charger Is Right for Your Business?

7kW single-phase chargers are ideal for workplace car parks where vehicles park overnight or for extended periods — a standard EV battery gains roughly 30–40 miles of range per hour. 22kW three-phase chargers suit daytime workplaces with faster turnover — vehicles gain 60–80 miles per hour — but require a three-phase electricity supply. Rapid DC chargers (50kW+) are suited to fleet depots and high-footfall car parks where fast turnaround is essential. For most UK workplaces, 7kW or 22kW is the right choice.

Smart Features and Load Balancing — Why They Matter

smart commercial ev charger with load balancing — how to choose for uk business

Smart commercial EV chargers connect to a management platform via Wi-Fi, 4G or ethernet, enabling remote monitoring, usage reporting, scheduled charging and RFID access control. Load balancing automatically distributes available power across multiple chargers — preventing grid overload without requiring expensive supply upgrades. This is critical for fleet depots and multi-unit car park installations. Ensure your charger supports OCPP (Open Charge Point Protocol) to prevent vendor lock-in.

Tethered vs Untethered Commercial EV Chargers

Tethered chargers have a fixed cable attached — convenient for staff car parks where all vehicles will use the same connector type. Untethered chargers have a socket only — the driver uses their own cable, which suits public-facing or mixed-vehicle installations. Both types are WCS grant eligible when installed by an OZEV-approved company. Your installer will advise on the best option for your site.
