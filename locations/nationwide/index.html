WCS Grants for Nationwide Businesses — What You're Entitled To

Nationwide businesses can claim the Workplace Charging Scheme grant of up to £500 per socket (maximum 40 sockets per applicant). Your OZEV-approved installer handles the entire application process. The grant is available to any UK-registered business, charity or public sector organisation with off-street parking — including offices, warehouses, car parks and fleet depots.

DNO Notifications for EV Charger Installation in Nationwide

All commercial EV charger installations require notification to your Distribution Network Operator (DNO). Your OZEV-approved installer handles the formal DNO notification as part of the installation process. For larger commercial installations (22kW+) or fleet depots, a formal grid connection assessment may be required. All installers listed above are experienced in managing DNO notifications for commercial nationwide ev charger installation projects.
